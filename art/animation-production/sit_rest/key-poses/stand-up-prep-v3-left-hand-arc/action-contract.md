# sit_rest stand-up v3 left-hand arc contract

## Revision reason

- User feedback: the v2 hand changes wrist/finger shape but remains visually fixed in roughly the same viewer-right screen region.
- v3 correction: move the anatomical left hand through four clearly separated contact/position zones driven by shoulder and elbow motion.

## Left-hand path

| Frame | Screen-space zone | Shoulder/elbow action | Contact |
|---:|---|---|---|
| F19 | inward and upward from the outer side, near viewer-right knee | shoulder adducts, elbow bends | hand approaches knee without contact |
| F20 | on the skirt-covered viewer-right knee/upper thigh, inside the satchel contour | palm presses down, elbow bears load | firm knee support |
| F21 | high viewer-right chest/shoulder zone | elbow lifts and bends; forearm swings forward/up | releases knee, open palm in air |
| F22 | far viewer-right side/back at waist-to-lower-chest height | shoulder opens, elbow softens | counterbalance follow-through |

## Locks

- Anatomical right hand on viewer-left keeps holding the strap throughout.
- Both shoe soles remain planted through F19-F22.
- The dress is one continuous garment with one uninterrupted lace hem.
- Left hand never grips/intersects the satchel or rabbit and never duplicates.
- Full-body drawing per frame; no compositing, interpolation, optical flow, morphing or local part movement.
